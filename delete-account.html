Account & Data Deletion Portal

Permanently delete your StudyTimer account, cloud backups, and study streaks.
Permanent Action: Deleting your account will immediately and permanently erase all cloud backups, study streaks, custom subjects, and email records. This cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the StudyTimer app on your Android phone, you can instantly erase your data without waiting:

  1. Open StudyTimer on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Settings (Gear icon) on the top bar.
  3. Tap your User Profile & Account section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm and tap PERMANENTLY DELETE.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you uninstalled the app or lost your device, submit your registered account email below. Our support team will permanently erase your remote records within 48 hours. For assistance, reach out via our Contact Support page.
