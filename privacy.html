Privacy Policy for StudyTimer

Last updated: September 3, 2026 • Effective Date: August 14, 2026

At StudyTimer ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to respecting and safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use the StudyTimer Android application and our associated web services, how that information is used, stored, and the rights you hold regarding your personal study data.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only process data necessary to provide and sync your study sessions:

  • Account Identifiers: When you choose to sign in via Google Authentication, we receive your basic profile identifiers (display name, email address, and profile picture avatar) solely to create and authenticate your account.
  • Study & Focus Records: Timestamps of study sessions, Pomodoro intervals, custom subject names, color tags, daily streak numbers, and target study goals.
  • Crash & Diagnostic Telemetry: Anonymized installation IDs and system logs to identify app crashes and improve stability across Android OS versions.

2. How We Store Your Data

StudyTimer utilizes an offline-first hybrid architecture:

  • Local Device Storage: All timer sessions and subject targets are stored locally using an encrypted SQLite/Room database on your device. You can use 100% of the app completely offline.
  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud synchronization with your Google Account, your data is transmitted securely over HTTPS (TLS 1.3) and stored in our Supabase PostgreSQL database with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

3. Third-Party Services & Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We use trusted infrastructure providers solely to support app functionality:

  • Supabase (Authentication & Database): For secure user authentication and cloud database sync.
  • Google Play Services: For app distribution, updates, and core Android lifecycle management.
  • Vercel: For hosting web portals and anonymous aggregated web analytics.

4. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud backups at any time:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Navigate to Settings > User Profile > Delete Account & Data. This triggers an automated cascading wipe of all cloud and local records.
  • Web Deletion Portal: If you no longer have the app installed, visit our dedicated Account Deletion Portal or email us directly at studytimer737@gmail.com. Requests are fulfilled within 48 hours.

5. Children's Privacy

StudyTimer does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly purge such data.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
