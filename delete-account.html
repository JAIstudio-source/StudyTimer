Account & Data Deletion Portal

Request permanent erasure of your StudyTimer account, cloud backups, and personal data.
Permanent Action: Deleting your account will immediately and permanently erase all cloud sync records, study streak metrics, habit logs, custom subjects, and account identifiers. This action cannot be reversed.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the StudyTimer Android application installed, you can trigger an immediate, automated self-service cascading deletion without waiting:

  1. Open the StudyTimer app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear icon) on the top left.
  3. Tap your User Profile & Account card.
  4. Scroll to the bottom to Account Actions & Deletion and tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation prompt and tap PERMANENTLY DELETE.

This instantly executes a cascading delete across your Supabase cloud storage and wipes all local Room databases.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, submit your registered account details below. Our support team will process and permanently purge all remote records associated with your email within 48 hours.

✓ Request Received: Your deletion request has been submitted. A confirmation email will be sent to your address once all cloud records have been permanently wiped from Supabase.

What Data Is Erased During Deletion?

  • Account Credentials: User ID, linked Google account tokens, and display names.
  • Session History: All past study sessions, break logs, and timeline timestamps.
  • Subject Tags & Goals: Custom subjects, color tags, and daily target parameters.
  • Cloud Storage: Custom profile avatar images stored in Supabase storage buckets.
  • Telemetry Data: The anonymous installation UUID is detached from any historical logs.
