About StudyTimer

Built for Students Everywhere • 100% Free & Ad-Free

StudyTimer was created with a simple mission: to give students a distraction-free, reliable study timer that doesn't bombard them with ads, lock basic features behind paywalls, or collect personal data.

Why We Built StudyTimer

Too many study apps on the Play Store are filled with annoying video popup ads, paywalls for basic charts, or require an internet connection just to start a 25-minute timer.

We believe that education and deep focus tools should be accessible to every student, everywhere:

  • Zero Ads: No distractions when you're trying to solve problems or prepare for tests.
  • Works 100% Offline: Study in library basements, on airplanes, or without Wi-Fi.
  • Color-Coded Subjects: Keep your Math, Physics, Coding, and History revisions organized.
  • Daily Study Streaks: Build unstoppable daily habits and celebrate your study consistency.

Open Source & Community Driven

StudyTimer is an open project built for the Android ecosystem. You can follow updates, download releases, and inspect the project on GitHub.
