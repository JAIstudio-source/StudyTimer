# StudyTimer 2.0.0 (versionCode 12)

What's New in This Update 🚀
Secure Cloud Sync: You can now sign in to safely back up your data and restore it anytime, anywhere.

Habit & Lecture Planner: Stay on track with dedicated planners built specifically to organize your daily routines and study schedule.

Dedicated Lecture Mode: Designed for students with fixed-time classes, helping you focus seamlessly when it matters most.

Performance Boosts & Bug Fixes: We’ve fine-tuned the app to run faster, smoother, and more reliably than ever before

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)
- Signed with official release key.

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v2.0.0). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
