# Privacy Policy - StudyTimer

**Effective Date:** August 15, 2026

StudyTimer is built with privacy by design. We respect your focus, personal time, and data integrity. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal technical data is collected, how it is protected, and your rights to manage or delete your data.

---

## 1. Zero Collection of Personal Notes & Task Contents
StudyTimer strictly practices **data minimization**:
- We **NEVER** collect, read, or upload your private task names, study notes, or custom checklist texts.
- We **NEVER** access your contacts, GPS location, microphone, camera, photos, or SMS messages.
- We **NEVER** use hardware fingerprinting, IMEI, MAC address, or Android device IDs.

---

## 2. Anonymous Telemetry & Usage Metrics
To measure active user growth and optimize timer stability, the app generates a random, privacy-safe **Anonymous UUID** on installation. We collect high-level functional metrics including:
- Active status (DAU, WAU, MAU cohorts).
- Instant 1st launch telemetry report upon fresh installation.
- Aggregated study duration and timer mode preferences (Stopwatch, Pomodoro, Scheduled Lecture).
- App version, Android OS version, and device model.
- Crash reports: stack traces with all email addresses and tokens strictly scrubbed.

---

## 3. Optional Account & Cloud Backup
Account creation is **strictly optional**. If you choose to sign in with Google or Email:
- Your account ID is linked to your sync profile for backing up your study streak and habit settings.
- All transmissions occur over secure encrypted HTTPS connections to Supabase cloud infrastructure.

---

## 4. True In-App Account & Data Deletion
You maintain complete ownership of your data. In the app settings under **Account -> Delete Account & Cloud Data**, you can permanently erase your cloud backups, habit history, profile information, and local databases.

---

## 5. Contact Us
For questions or data deletion requests, contact us at our official GitHub repository: [github.com/JAIstudio-source/StudyTimer](https://github.com/JAIstudio-source/StudyTimer).
