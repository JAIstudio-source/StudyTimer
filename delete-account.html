Account & Data Deletion Portal

Request permanent erasure of your StudyTimer account, cloud sync databases, and study metrics.
Permanent & Irreversible Action: Deleting your account immediately erases all cloud backups, habit streaks, custom subject categories, and Google authentication identifiers. This action cannot be undone.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the StudyTimer Android application installed, you can trigger an immediate automated cascading deletion without waiting:

  1. Open the StudyTimer app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear icon) on the top bar.
  3. Tap your User Profile & Account card.
  4. Scroll to Account Actions and tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation prompt and tap PERMANENTLY DELETE.

This instantly executes a cascading delete across your Supabase cloud storage and wipes all local Room databases.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, submit your registered account details below. Our support team will process and permanently purge all remote records associated with your email within 48 hours.

✓ Request Submitted Successfully

Your deletion request has been logged. All cloud backups and associated tokens for your email will be permanently purged from Supabase within 48 hours. A confirmation notification will be sent to your email.

What Data Is Erased During Deletion?

  • Account Identifiers: Supabase Auth User ID, linked Google email, and display avatar tokens.
  • Focus History: All past study sessions, break logs, and timeline timestamps.
  • Subject Tags & Goals: Custom subject categories, color tags, and daily target parameters.
  • Cloud Storage: Custom profile avatar images stored in cloud storage buckets.
  • Telemetry: Anonymous installation UUIDs are detached and purged from historical logs.
