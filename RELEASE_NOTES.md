# StudyTimer 3.3.7 (versionCode 51)

### What's New in Version 3.3.7 🚀
- **Planner Goal Interaction Fix**: Fixed goal completion persistence so ticking goals with time remaining preserves the checkmark and progress bar without reverting to deficit.
- **Daily Goal Rollover & History Protection**: Protected user-configured daily goals from resetting to 45m on app update, with automatic self-healing for past historical records.
- **Timer Mode Pill Visibility**: Mode switch button now cleanly and instantly hides while a session is active (studying, on break, or paused) and only shows when the timer is idle.
- **Performance & Stability**: Smoother live stat syncing and updated unit testing coverage.

---

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v3.3.7). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
