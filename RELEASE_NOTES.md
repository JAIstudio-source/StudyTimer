# StudyTimer 2.5.7 (versionCode 18)

What's New in Version 2.5.7 🚀
- **Subject Pie Chart & Detailed Session Analytics**: Added interactive pie chart visualizations for subject session distribution and focus depth ratios (Deep Focus ≥40m, Standard Focus 15-40m, Light Focus <15m) with date navigation.
- **Ambient Sound Engine**: Integrated relaxing background soundscapes (Rain, Birds, Lo-Fi beats) to boost concentration during timer sessions.
- **Celebration Animations**: Added celebratory reward animations for reaching daily focus goals and building study streaks.

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)
- Signed with official release key.

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v2.5.7). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
