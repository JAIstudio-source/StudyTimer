# StudyTimer 3.2.0 (versionCode 43)

### What's New in Version 3.2.0 🚀
- **Study Rooms:** Create or join study rooms to study together with friends in real time.
- **User Profiles:** Tap on anyone in rooms or the leaderboard to view their profile and stats.
- **Swipe to Refresh:** Easily refresh the leaderboard by pulling down from the top.
- **Better Alerts:** Fixed popup messages repeating over and over when tapping buttons quickly.
- **Cleaner Planner:** Improved goal history and simplified yesterday's goals.
- **Bug Fixes:** Various improvements for a smoother and faster experience.

---

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v3.2.0). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
