# About StudyTimer

> Distraction-Free Android Focus Companion &bull; Offline-First Architecture &bull; 100% Free &amp; Ad-Free

## Mission & Philosophy
StudyTimer was engineered to help students, self-taught developers, and exam candidates build sustained cognitive flow without digital distractions. Unlike mainstream timer apps overloaded with video ads, intrusive subscriptions, and complex menus, StudyTimer is 100% free forever and ad-free.

## Core Principles
1. **100% Free Forever:** All features, analytics, custom subjects, and Pomodoro modes are fully unlocked with zero subscriptions.
2. **Zero Advertisements:** No banner ads or video interstitials that break focus streaks.
3. **Offline-First:** All data writes to a local SQLite/Room database on device.
4. **Cloud Backup (Optional):** Encrypted Google Cloud sync backed by Supabase PostgreSQL.
5. **Data Ownership:** You can wipe all data anytime via the app or https://get-studytimer.vercel.app/delete-account.html.
