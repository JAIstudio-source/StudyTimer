About StudyTimer

Built for Students Everywhere • 100% Free & Ad-Free

StudyTimer was created with a simple mission: to give students a distraction-free, reliable study timer that doesn't bombard them with ads, lock basic features behind paywalls, or harvest personal data.

Why We Built StudyTimer

Too many study apps on mobile stores are filled with intrusive popup ads, paywalls for basic charts, or require constant internet connectivity just to start a 25-minute Pomodoro timer.

We believe that education and deep focus tools should be accessible to every student, everywhere:

  • Zero Ads & Zero Paywalls: No distractions when you're solving problem sets or preparing for board exams.
  • Works 100% Offline: Study in library basements, on airplanes, or with your phone on airplane mode.
  • Color-Coded Subjects: Keep your Math, Physics, Coding, and History revisions organized.
  • Daily Study Streaks: Build unstoppable daily habits and celebrate your study consistency.
  • Cross-Platform Synchronization: Seamlessly study on our Online Web Studio or our native Android app.

Open Source & Community Driven

StudyTimer is an open project built for students worldwide. You can follow updates, download releases, and inspect the project on GitHub, or reach out directly through our contact page.
