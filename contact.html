2. Support Response Standards (SLA)

We treat all user communications with high priority:

  • Standard Turnaround: Responses within 24 hours on business days and weekends.
  • Critical Bug Reports: Investigated and patched within 48-72 hours via APK hotfix releases.
  • Data Deletion Requests: Processed and permanently purged within 48 hours of verification.

3. How to Submit Effective Bug Reports

To help our Android engineers resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Device Model & Android Version: E.g., Samsung Galaxy S23, Google Pixel 8 running Android 14.
  • App Version: Located at the bottom of the in-app Settings screen (e.g., v3.0.0).
  • Reproduction Steps: A brief description of what you were doing when the issue occurred.
  • Screenshots / Error Messages: Any relevant screen capture or crash details.
