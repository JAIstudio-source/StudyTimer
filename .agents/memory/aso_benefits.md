# StudyTimer ASO Benefits & Metadata

## App Overview
- **App Name:** StudyTimer (100% Free & Offline Focus Companion)
- **Package ID:** `com.madeby.JAI`
- **Target Audience:** Students, self-learners, exam aspirants (UPSC, JEE, NEET, GRE, MCAT), and deep-work professionals.
- **Unique Selling Proposition (USP):** 100% offline, zero ads, complete privacy with local Room database, custom focus dial, habit planner, study heatmaps, and weekly shareable progress cards.
- **Style Direction:** Clean minimalist pastel/off-white background, 3D floating layered structure, high-end mobile mockup, crisp modern typography.
- **Target Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 Portrait (Play Store ready)
