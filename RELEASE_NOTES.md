# StudyTimer 3.3.4 (versionCode 48)

- **Clearer Daily Goals:** See remaining study time at a glance with theme-matched colors, time left always visible, and distinct completion states.

Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v3.3.4). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
