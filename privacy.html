🔒 Data Safety & Student Privacy

Privacy Policy for StudyTimer

Effective Date: September 2026 • Free Android Study Companion

At StudyTimer, we believe in minimal data collection and zero privacy invasion. We built StudyTimer to help students focus on their homework, exams, and habits without tracking their personal lives.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect unnecessary personal data. We only process data required to keep your study sessions running smoothly:

  • Study Logs & Streaks: Timestamps of study sessions, Pomodoro intervals, custom subject names, color tags, daily streaks, and target study goals.
  • Google Account (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud backups, we receive your basic email identifier solely to connect your private backup database.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Anonymous system reports to fix bugs on different Android phone models.

2. Where Your Study Data Is Stored

StudyTimer is built with an offline-first architecture:

  • On Your Android Phone: 100% of your study timers, subjects, and calendar streaks are stored locally on your device in a private offline database. The app works completely offline.
  • Cloud Backup (Optional): If you turn on cloud sync, your data is sent securely using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) to keep your study records safe if you switch devices.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We do not sell, rent, or trade student data. There are zero third-party advertisements or tracking trackers inside the StudyTimer Android app.

4. Deleting Your Data

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete all local data at any time by clearing app data in Android Settings, or use our Account & Data Deletion Portal.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, please email us directly at studytimer737@gmail.com.
