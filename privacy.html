Privacy Policy for StudyTimer

Last Updated: September 2026 • Free Android Study Companion & Web Studio

At StudyTimer, we believe student focus tools should protect user privacy, not exploit it. We built StudyTimer from the ground up with a strict offline-first architecture, zero third-party advertising trackers, and transparent data practices. To understand our mission and philosophy, read About StudyTimer.

1. Core Architecture: Offline-First by Design

StudyTimer operates primarily on your device without requiring continuous internet connectivity or mandatory account registration:

  • On Your Android Device: 100% of your study timers, custom subjects, color tags, Pomodoro intervals, and daily streaks are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database. You can use the full application completely offline without creating an account or sharing any personal information.
  • On StudyTimer Web Studio: If you use our browser-based Web Studio, study sessions and preferences are stored locally in your browser's private IndexedDB and LocalStorage cache.
  • Optional Cloud Synchronization: Cloud features (such as multi-device sync, leaderboard rankings, and point-in-time data recovery) are completely optional and only activated when you explicitly sign in or enable them.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Used

We practice strict data minimization. We only process data necessary to provide core study features:

  • Study Logs & Metrics: Session start/end timestamps, elapsed study duration, subject titles, goal targets, and streak records.
  • Account Identifiers (Optional): If you choose to sign in with Google or Email for cloud backups, we store your unique user ID and email strictly to authenticate and secure your private data vault.
  • Public Student Profile (Opt-In): If you customize your student profile, your chosen display name, username, bio, study goal/exam, and optional avatar image are stored in our secure database. All profile bios and names pass through an automated profanity filter to maintain a safe, student-friendly environment.
  • Leaderboard & Live Status (Opt-In): If you enable public leaderboard participation or live study status, your current session duration and subject tag are visible to other students in the community. You can disable this visibility at any time in Settings.
  • Fair-Play & Anti-Cheat Telemetry: To maintain genuine student motivation on public leaderboards, the system verifies study streak integrity and uses automated inactivity checks for long continuous sessions (preventing unattended 24/7 background running).

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising & No Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade student data with any third-party brokers or advertisers.

  • The StudyTimer Android app contains zero third-party advertisement SDKs (no AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or data broker trackers).
  • We do not perform cross-app tracking, device fingerprinting, or behavioral profiling for commercial advertising.
  • Cloud database infrastructure is hosted securely on Supabase PostgreSQL with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies and HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Compliance with the DPDP Act, 2023 (Digital Personal Data Protection Act)

StudyTimer fully complies with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and global privacy standards regarding the processing of digital personal data.

🛡️ Your Legal Rights under DPDP Act 2023:

  • Right to Access & Summary: You have the right to view a complete summary of all personal study data and profile information stored in our systems at any time directly through the app or web interface.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You can edit, modify, or correct your profile details, avatar, study goals, or session tags instantly via the profile editor.
  • Right to Erasure & Withdrawal of Consent: You have the unconditional right to withdraw consent and delete your digital personal data. You can delete your account and all associated cloud records in 1-click via our Self-Serve Account Deletion Portal or within app Settings.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal provided by our Data Protection & Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: In the event of death or incapacity, a user or their lawful representative may nominate an individual to exercise their data protection rights.

5. Protection of Children & Minor Students

Recognizing that many StudyTimer users are high school and college students, we implement special safeguards in accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act:

  • We do not engage in targeted advertising directed at minors or students.
  • We do not track or profile children’s browsing behaviors across the web.
  • We enforce automated moderation and profanity filters across all student usernames and bios to ensure a safe learning space.

6. Data Retention & Point-in-Time Backups

Local study logs remain on your Android device for as long as the application remains installed. When cloud sync is enabled, snapshots are securely stored to allow students to restore their data if they change devices. If you submit a deletion request via our Account Deletion Portal, your cloud profile, study backups, and leaderboard entries are immediately and permanently erased from our production servers.

7. Designated Grievance Redressal Officer

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023 and Information Technology rules, if you have any questions, feedback, concerns, or grievances regarding the processing of your personal data or wish to exercise any of your data rights, you may contact our designated Grievance Officer:
