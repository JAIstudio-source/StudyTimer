Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026 • Version 2.4.0

At StudyTimer, your privacy and data autonomy are our core priorities. We build tools to help you focus, manage study sessions, and achieve your academic goals without tracking your personal life. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal technical data is collected, how it is stored, and your rights to manage or delete your data.

The Core Pledge: We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or study logs to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties.

1. Zero Collection of Personal Notes & Private Content

StudyTimer strictly practices data minimization:

  • We NEVER access or upload your private study notes, journal text, camera, microphone, photos, media files, or contact lists.
  • We NEVER collect personal identifiers such as GPS location, IMEI, MAC address, phone numbers, or hardware fingerprints.
  • Your study subject names, schedules, and custom goals remain strictly private to you.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Used

To provide reliable timer functions and sync across devices, we process:

  • Local Study Logs & Stats: Focus session duration, break durations, dates, completed Pomodoro counts, and subject tags stored locally on your device database (Room/SQLite).
  • Optional Cloud Sync Data: If you sign in (e.g. Google Sign-In or Email authentication via Supabase), your encrypted session logs, streak counts, and theme settings are backed up to secure Supabase cloud servers.
  • Anonymized Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry: Optional anonymous metrics (e.g. daily active status, app version, Android OS version, device model, and scrubbed crash traces) to maintain app stability and eliminate bugs.

3. Android System Permissions & Rationale

StudyTimer requests only essential system permissions:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS - Android 13+): Used strictly to display the persistent timer notification and alert you with sound/vibration when your focus session or break ends.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Keeps your study session timer actively counting in the background when your screen is turned off or you switch apps.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Required for optional cloud backups, account authentication, and sending feedback reports.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

We work exclusively with industry-standard, privacy-compliant cloud providers:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL Cloud Infrastructure): Encrypted database and storage for optional user account authentication and cloud backups.
  • Vercel: Fast, secure hosting for the official website, release updates, and serverless feedback endpoints.

5. Account & Complete Data Deletion Rights

You have full legal rights to permanently delete your data at any time:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Open the app → Settings → Account & Profile → Delete Account & Data → Type DELETE to instantly wipe all remote cloud documents, local Room databases, and preferences.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you cannot access the app, visit our Account Deletion Web Portal or submit a manual deletion request to support.
