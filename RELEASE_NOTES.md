# StudyTimer 3.2.0 (versionCode 43)

### What's New in Version 3.2.0 🚀
- **Live Study Rooms & Circles:** Study together in real time, view active peers, and share room invite links with one tap.
- **Member Profile Cards:** Tap any member in study rooms or leaderboard to view their bio, focus stats, and badges.
- **Leaderboard Polish:** Added smooth pull-down-to-refresh with intelligent server rate-limiting and optimized space.
- **Smart Toast Manager (AppToast):** Fixed message stacking/queuing on rapid taps—toasts now cancel duplicates and display cleanly.
- **Planner Refinements:** Streamlined goal history and yesterday's goals workflow.
- **Cloud Sync & Performance:** Hardened cloud synchronization and database queries.

---

## Compatibility
- Android 9 (API 28) through Android 16 (API 36)

## Installation
Install `StudyTimer-release.apk` (v3.2.0). Updating preserves all your study logs, streaks, and settings.
